# Content standard

## Positioning

Lee Groenewegen is a **Shopify Plus technical and transformation leader**: Tech Lead at fusefabric (Shopify Platinum Partner), leading enterprise programmes and teams. He is **not** presented as a developer for hire.

Lead with, in this order:
1. Technical and transformation leadership: programmes, teams, stakeholders, partners
2. Enterprise architecture: multi-market, shared themes, integrations, extensions
3. Delivery governance: Git workflows, CI/CD, releases, standards
4. AI in commerce delivery: agent-ready structure, governance, AI-assisted workflows

Hands-on technical skills are supporting evidence only (for example, on the Experience page's capabilities list).

**Audiences:** enterprise brands, Shopify partners, and recruiters hiring for roles such as *Shopify Transformation Lead* or *Shopify Plus Technical Lead*.

## Voice

- Plain, confident and specific. Short sentences. First person ("I lead…").
- British English: optimise, programme, colour, organisation.
- No hype words: amazing, incredible, dreams, passionate, cutting-edge, seamless, world-class.
- No emoji. No exclamation marks.
- Headings state a point ("Structure first, so teams can move fast"), not a category ("Our Services").

## Facts policy

- **Never invent** metrics, percentages, client names, testimonials, dates, team sizes or outcomes.
- Verified facts live in [cv.html](../cv.html) and [.ai/project-context.yaml](../.ai/project-context.yaml). Use only those, or facts Lee gives in the current task.
- If copy would be stronger with a fact you don't have, write it without the fact and **list the gap** in your handoff.
- When you rephrase a fact, keep its meaning. Don't upgrade "worked on" to "led".

## Needs Lee's explicit approval

- Naming a new client or programme (client confidentiality)
- Any statement about availability, rates or contract work (Lee is employed at fusefabric)
- New claims about scale, results or seniority
- Removing an existing client, role or credential
