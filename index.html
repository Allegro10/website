What I do

Where technology, delivery and the business meet.

The hard part of a Shopify Plus programme is rarely the code. It's aligning stakeholders, partners and teams around an architecture that will still work after launch.

Transformation leadership

Owning Shopify Plus programmes from roadmap to go-live: bridging commercial requirements and technology, and coordinating internal teams, third parties and implementation partners.

Enterprise architecture

Multi-market and multi-store structures, shared themes, integrations, checkout and admin extensions, and the release workflows that let large teams ship safely.

AI in commerce delivery

Helping teams adopt AI agents with control: structure that agents can work with, guardrails for shared code, and clear ownership of what changed and why.
